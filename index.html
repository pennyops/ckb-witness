ckb-witness: public CKB RPC agreement monitor

Each hourly run asks every keyless public CKB mainnet RPC for its tip, then compares the block hash at a common height (tip - 12). Disagreement or a lagging endpoint means a user who trusts that single endpoint could see a stale or different chain view. Source and raw log: github.com/pennyops/ckb-witness (measurements.log).
